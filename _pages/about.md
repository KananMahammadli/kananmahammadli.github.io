Hi! I am an incoming PhD student at the [CS@Max Planck Doctoral Program](https://www.cis.mpg.de/csmaxplanck-overview/). I recently completed my B.Sc. in Mathematics (with a Minor in Statistics) at [Middle East Technical University (METU)](https://www.metu.edu.tr/), where I graduated with High Honors.

My research interests lie at the intersection of deep learning, computer vision, and representation learning. I am particularly motivated by the challenge of building principled and robust methods for structured perception and generalization in vision and language models — foundations for scalable and trustworthy AI systems.

### Research Interests

- Robust and structured generalization in vision and language models  
- Representation learning across multimodal and hierarchical tasks  
- Deep learning for structured prediction and adaptive perception  

I have had the opportunity to work on academic research projects in machine learning and computer vision, under the guidance of:
- [Prof. Seyda Ertekin](https://avesis.metu.edu.tr/sertekin) at METU, on Large Language Model-based Hyper-parameter Optimization  
- [Prof. Ioannis Kakadiaris](https://uh.edu/cbl/people/about-director.php) at the University of Houston, on representation learning and object detection  
- [Prof. Yukun Hu](https://www.ucl.ac.uk) at University College London, on physics-informed deep learning

These experiences have shaped my long-term goal: to advance the science behind adaptive and efficient perception systems capable of learning continually and transferring knowledge across tasks and domains.
