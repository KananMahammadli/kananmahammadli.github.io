# Preprints

* **Sequential Large Language Model-Based Hyper-parameter Optimization (SLLMBO)**  
  **Kanan Mahammadli**, Seyda Ertekin  
  *arXiv preprint, 2024* · [View on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20302)  
  - Developed SLLMBO, a sequential hyperparameter-optimization framework combining LLM-guided configuration proposals with Tree-structured Parzen Estimation.
  - Evaluated four LLMs across 14 classification and regression tasks; the proposed LLM-TPE sampler achieved better results than the evaluated Bayesian-optimization methods on 9 tasks.

* **Class-Specific Data Augmentation: Bridging the Imbalance in Multiclass Breast Cancer Classification**  
  **Kanan Mahammadli**, Abdullah Burkan Bereketoglu, Ayse Gul Kabakci  
  *arXiv preprint, 2023* · [View on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.09981)  
  - Studied how applying a uniform augmentation strategy across classes, together with relying on aggregate evaluation metrics, can obscure degraded performance on minority classes.
  - Developed a class-specific augmentation pipeline using stain normalization and a transfer-learning Vision Transformer, improving minority-class performance on the BreaKHis dataset relative to the evaluated baseline models.
