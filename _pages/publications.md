# Publications

* **Sequential Large Language Model-Based Hyperparameter Optimization (SLLMBO)**  
  **Kanan Mahammadli**, Seyda Ertekin  
  *arXiv preprint, 2024*  
  [View on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20302)  
  - Introduced the SLLMBO framework for large language model-guided hyperparameter optimization.
  - Developed a hybrid LLM-TPE sampler that improved over fully LLM-based optimization strategies across a range of benchmark tasks.
  - Studied the strengths and limitations of different LLMs in sequential optimization settings.
