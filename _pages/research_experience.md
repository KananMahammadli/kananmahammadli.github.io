# Research Experience

* **Doctoral Researcher, [Max Planck Institute for Informatics](https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/home)** (Oct. 2025 – Jul. 2026)
  * Completed two Research Immersion Labs under the supervision of [Prof. Jan Eric Lenssen](https://janericlenssen.github.io/) and [Dr. Marc Habermann](https://people.mpi-inf.mpg.de/~mhaberma/).
  * Investigated self-supervised continuous latent-action representations learned from human videos and their potential use in downstream robot action generation.
  * Studied whether compact representations of visual transitions preserve the task-relevant information required for sequential decisions.
  * Evaluated learned representations through reconstruction, retrieval, and robot-manipulation experiments, using representation learning together with diffusion- and flow-matching-based action modeling.
  * This work motivated my broader interest in representation sufficiency, long-horizon decision-making, compositional generalization, and failure analysis in learned autonomous systems.

* **Undergraduate Student Researcher, [AI Lab, METU BILTIR Center](https://biltir.metu.edu.tr/)** (Jul. 2023 – Jun. 2025)
  * Supervisor: [Prof. Seyda Ertekin](https://avesis.metu.edu.tr/sertekin)
  * Developed SLLMBO, a sequential hyperparameter-optimization framework combining LLM-guided configuration proposals with Tree-structured Parzen Estimation.
  * Evaluated four LLMs across 14 classification and regression tasks; the proposed LLM-TPE sampler achieved better results than the evaluated Bayesian-optimization methods on 9 tasks.

* **Computer Vision Research Intern, [Computational Biomedicine Lab, University of Houston](https://uh.edu/cbl/)** (Jul. 2022 – Sep. 2022)
  * Supervisor: [Prof. Ioannis Kakadiaris](https://uh.edu/cbl/people/about-director.php)
  * Worked on human detection from UAV imagery under varying viewpoints, distances, and weather conditions using feature pyramid networks.
  * Worked on face representation and clustering using FaceNet embeddings and FAISS for efficient similarity-based analysis.
